# Forward Deployed Engineer for AI Systems

Here is a clean, structured 4-week study plan designed to help you master the course **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) for AI Systems**.

This plan breaks down the 11 modules into manageable daily and weekly targets, balancing **core delivery & consulting skills**, **deep technical AI system architecture**, **practical case studies/labs**, and **interview preparation**.

---

## 📅 Course Overview & Target Schedule

| Week       | Phase / Focus Area                                   | Modules Covered     | Primary Goal                                                                                                |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Week 1** | **Foundations & Discovery to POC**                   | Modules 1, 2, & 3   | Master the FDE role, discovery, scoping, POC sprints, and production handoffs.                              |
| **Week 2** | **Enterprise Constraints & AI Systems Architecture** | Modules 4 & 5       | Learn harness engineering, guardrails, memory, RAG, and multi-agent orchestration.                          |
| **Week 3** | **Real-World Case Studies & Hands-on Projects**      | Modules 7, 8, & 9   | Deconstruct RAG, multi-agent rerouting, image fine-tuning, and complete the Cloud Lab.                      |
| **Week 4** | **Interview Mastery & Final Wrap-Up**                | Modules 6, 10, & 11 | Prepare for company-specific FDE interviews (Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) & claim your certificate. |

---

## 🛠️ Week-by-Week Breakdown

### **Week 1: FDE Lifecycle — Discovery, Scoping & Production Delivery**

_Focus: Master the consulting, delivery, and project management capabilities of a Forward Deployed Engineer._

- **Day 1: Role Foundations**
- Module 1: _Who Is a Forward Deployed Engineer?_ & _Are You Ready for This Course?_
- Understand the intersection of AI engineering, customer empathy, and field implementation.

- **Day 2: Customer Discovery & Workflow Identification**
- Module 2: _Running Customer Discovery_
- Learn techniques to uncover root operational problems vs. feature requests; gather actionable data.

- **Day 3: Solution Architecture & Project Scoping**
- Module 2: _Scoping and Solution Architecture_ & _The Proof of Concept (POC) Sprint_
- Craft technical scope documents, align goals with constraints, and manage fast-paced 2–4 week POC sprints.

- **Day 4: Transition to Production**
- Module 3: _The Pilot Phase_ & _The Project Handoff_
- Evaluate system readiness, establish metrics, gather user feedback, and transition ownership to client teams.

- **Day 5: Field Feedback & Review**
- Module 3: _The Field Feedback Loop_
- **Weekly Review & Quizzes:** Complete Module 1–3 Quizzes to lock in lifecycle concepts.

---

### **Week 2: Enterprise Constraints & AI Systems Architecture**

_Focus: Gain deep technical knowledge on LLM systems, guardrails, memory, and agent orchestration._

- **Day 6: Enterprise Constraints & Stakeholders**
- Module 4: _Navigating Enterprise Constraints_ & _Communicating Across Stakeholder Types_
- Learn to handle security/privacy requirements, legacy infrastructure, executive briefings, and developer enablement.

- **Day 7: The AI System Landscape & LLMs**
- Module 5: _The AI System Landscape_ & _LLM-Based Systems_
- Explore modern LLM deployment patterns, context windows, and latency/cost trade-offs.

- **Day 8: Harness Engineering (Context, Memory & Guardrails)**
- Module 5: _Harness Engineering: Tools, Memory, and Context_ & _Guardrails, Observability, and Review_
- Master tool integration, vector storage/retrieval, input/output validation, and trace observability.

- **Day 9: Agentic Frameworks & Multi-Agent Orchestration**
- Module 5: _Multi-Agent Orchestration Patterns_ & _Choosing an Agentic Framework_
- Evaluate frameworks (LangChain, AutoGen, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel) and patterns (hierarchical, sequential, swarm).

- **Day 10: System Architecture Review & Practice**
- **Weekly Review & Quizzes:** Complete Module 4 & Module 5 Quizzes and design diagrams for a sample agent architecture.

---

### **Week 3: Real-World Case Studies & Hands-on Implementation**

_Focus: Apply theory to 3 industry-standard enterprise case studies and complete the Cloud Lab._

- **Day 11–12: Case Study 1 — Financial Knowledge Assistant (RAG)**
- Module 7: _Decomposing the Problem_, _System Design_, and _FDE Interview Questions_
- **Project Work:** _Build a RAG Using LangChain with Google Gemini_

- **Day 13–14: Case Study 2 — Shipment Rerouting Agent (Multi-Agent System)**
- Module 8: _Decomposing the Problem_, _System Design_, and _FDE Interview Questions_
- **Project Work:** _Develop a Multi-Agent Research Assistant Using AutoGen_

- **Day 15–16: Case Study 3 — Hospital Billing System (Fine-Tuning & Computer Vision)**
- Module 9: _Decomposing the Problem_, _System Design_, and _FDE Interview Questions_
- **Cloud Lab:** _Fine-Tune a SageMaker JumpStart Model for Image Classification_

- **Day 17: Case Study Consolidation**
- Compare trade-offs between RAG vs. Fine-Tuning vs. Multi-Agent orchestration across the 3 case studies.

---

### **Week 4: FDE Interview Fundamentals & Company Guides**

_Focus: Prepare for top-tier AI company technical/field interview rounds and complete course requirements._

- **Day 18: FDE Interview Fundamentals**
- Module 6: _How FDE Interviews Work_ & _The Four Interview Rounds_
- System design, coding/implementation, behavioral/customer scenario, and architecture deep dives.

- **Day 19: Enterprise AI Interview Guides (Part 1)**
- Module 10: _Interview Guide: Palantir Forward Deployed AI Engineers_
- Module 10: _Interview Guide: OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineers_

- **Day 20: Enterprise AI Interview Guides (Part 2)**
- Module 10: _Interview Guide: Anthropic Applied AI Engineers_
- Module 10: _Interview Guide: Google Cloud GenAI FDE_

- **Day 21: Course Wrap-Up & Certification**
- Module 11: _What’s Next?_ & Final Quiz/Assessment Review
- **Claim Certificate of Completion** and update your resume/LinkedIn profile with FDE AI project achievements.

---

## 💡 Key Tips for Success

1. **Space Out the Quizzes:** Complete each section's quiz immediately after reading to ensure knowledge retention.
2. **Focus on Trade-offs:** In FDE roles, there is rarely one "right" answer—practice articulating _why_ you choose RAG over Fine-tuning, or AutoGen over LangChain based on customer constraints (cost, latency, data privacy).
3. **Hands-On Cloud Labs:** Dedicate uninterrupted 2-hour blocks for the **SageMaker JumpStart Cloud Lab** and the **Gemini / AutoGen projects**.
